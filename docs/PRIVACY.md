# Due Back Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 17, 2026**

This policy describes the current Due Back app, including its optional
Audiobookshelf connection, which is still being tested.

## The short version

Due Back has no account service or developer-operated server for your library.
Your library and reading information stays on your device unless you choose to
back it up to your USB drive, connect an Audiobookshelf server, or send us an
email.

## Information stored on your device

Due Back stores the information needed to run your library, such as book names
and details, your place in a book, notes, listening history, shelves you
create, and app settings. It may also store book files you check out or add to
the phone and small cover images used by your library.

Due Back does not currently include advertising or analytics services and
does not send this library information to a server operated by the Due Back
developer.

## USB-drive backup

When **Back up reading data to the drive** is turned on, Due Back copies
reading data to the USB drive you selected. This setting is on by default for
a library on a USB drive and can be turned off in Settings. The backup can
include book identifiers, reading positions, notes, characters and tags,
listening history, checkout history, and your shelves. It does not include the
audiobook audio files.

Anyone with access to that USB drive may be able to read its Due Back backup.
Keep the drive somewhere you trust.

## Android device backup

Android's own backup feature may also save app data, including library and
reading information, depending on your device's backup settings. Android
controls that backup and its retention. Due Back excludes checked-out book
copies and cover images from Android backup; those can be restored from the
original drive or made again by scanning the library.

## Optional Audiobookshelf connection

If you choose to connect a server, Due Back sends your sign-in details to the
server address you enter so the server can authenticate you. Due Back does not
keep your password; it stores the access credentials needed for later
connections in protected storage on your device.

The app requests library information and covers from that server. If you
choose to stream or download a book, the audio comes from that server. The app
also sends playback position and listening activity for server books back to
that server. The server is operated by you or its owner, not by the Due Back
developer, and its operator's privacy practices apply to information it
receives.

Connection security depends on the server address and network you use. Some
current testing builds allow unencrypted HTTP connections. Use a trusted
network and prefer HTTPS when your server supports it.

## Email and support

If you use the in-app problem or idea form, Due Back opens your email app with
your message. For a problem report, it also adds the Due Back app version and
Android version. Nothing is sent until you choose to send the email in your
email app. Your email provider then handles the message under its own privacy
policy.

You can also contact us at
[duebackapp@gmail.com](mailto:duebackapp@gmail.com).

## Website hosting

If this policy is viewed on a GitHub Pages website, GitHub hosts the page.
GitHub says it records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. That is
website hosting data and is separate from information stored by the Due Back
app. See [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## Your choices and deleting information

- You can turn USB-drive backup off in Due Back Settings.
- You can remove an Audiobookshelf connection in the app. This removes its
  saved sign-in credentials from the device; it does not delete data held by
  the Audiobookshelf server.
- You can remove Due Back's local data using your device's app settings or by
  uninstalling the app. This does not remove a backup already written to your
  USB drive. To remove that backup, delete the `.dueback` folder from the
  drive using a file manager.
- To ask us a privacy question, email
  [duebackapp@gmail.com](mailto:duebackapp@gmail.com).

## Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. The date at
the top shows when it was last revised.
