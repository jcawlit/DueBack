# Due Back

Due Back is an audiobook player for people who keep their collection on a USB
drive. Keep most of your books on the drive, check out a few to your phone,
and return them when you want the space back. Your place in each book stays
saved.

## How it works

Choose a folder of audiobook files and Due Back builds a library from their
book information. It does not copy every audio file just to make the library.
Check out books to listen offline, then return them to free phone storage
without losing your place, notes, or listening history.

The app is in development. Core USB-drive playback has been tested on an
Android phone with a real drive. The optional Audiobookshelf connection is
still experimental.
